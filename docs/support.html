Quick Help

App not responding or stuck loading?

Force-close the app and reopen it. If the problem persists, check that you have a working internet connection — AgileCoachIQ requires one to reach the AI.

I used all my free questions for today

The free tier includes 5 AI coaching questions per day. Your limit resets at midnight. To get unlimited questions, upgrade to AgileCoachIQ Pro ($9.99/month) in the app under Settings → Upgrade to Pro. You can also bring your own Anthropic API key in Settings for unlimited use at your own API cost.

Pro subscription not unlocking after purchase

Pull down to refresh on the home screen, or go to Settings → Restore Purchases. If it still doesn't unlock, force-close and reopen the app. Purchases are managed by Apple and may take a moment to propagate.

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple — not inside the app. To cancel:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad
  2. Tap your name → Subscriptions
  3. Tap AgileCoachIQ → Cancel Subscription

You keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds go through Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the AgileCoachIQ charge, and request a refund.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AgileCoachIQ?

AgileCoachIQ is an AI-powered Agile coaching app for Scrum Masters, Product Owners, Agile Coaches, and teams. It uses Claude (by Anthropic) to answer questions about Scrum, SAFe, Kanban, Lean, and Agile coaching — grounded in the 2020 Scrum Guide, Coaching Agile Teams, Management 3.0, the SAFe DevOps Workbook, and more.

What can AgileCoachIQ help me with?

  • AI Chat Coaching — ask anything about Agile, Scrum, SAFe, Lean, or coaching
  • 15+ Guided Tools — Sprint Planner, Retro Facilitator, OKR Builder, Team Health Check, PI Planning, Backlog Refinement, Stakeholder Updates, and more
  • Topic Browser — browse curated questions across Scrum, SAFe, Coaching, Ceremonies, Metrics, and Lean
  • Certification Prep — practice questions and mock exams for PSM I, PSM II, PSPO I, SAFe Agilist, CSM, CSPO
  • Board Analysis — upload a photo of your Kanban or Jira board for AI coaching feedback
  • Conversation History — every session is saved and searchable

How does the free tier work?

Free users get 5 AI coaching questions per day. All guided tools, the topic browser, conversation history, and Sprint Notes are fully available at no cost. The daily limit resets at midnight.

What is "Bring Your Own Key"?

If you have an Anthropic API key, you can enter it in Settings → API Key. Your key is stored securely on your device and never sent to our servers. This gives you unlimited questions billed directly to your Anthropic account — no subscription needed.

Is my data private?

Your conversations are stored locally on your device. Your API key (if you provide one) is stored in your device's secure keychain. We do not collect or store your chat history on our servers. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Does the app work offline?

The app requires an internet connection to send questions to the AI. Previously saved conversations are accessible offline through the History tab.

Which AI model does AgileCoachIQ use?

AgileCoachIQ uses Anthropic's Claude models — the same family powering Claude.ai. Responses are grounded in established Agile frameworks and literature, not generic web content.

The AI gave me an answer I disagree with — what should I do?

AI responses are a starting point, not a prescription. If something doesn't match your context or you believe it's incorrect, follow up in the same conversation — the AI maintains context and can refine its answer. For important decisions, always validate against the official Scrum Guide, SAFe framework, or your organization's processes.
